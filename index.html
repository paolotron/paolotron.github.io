Paolo Rabino

Research Lead @ mkind · PhD in AI @ Politecnico di Torino

I build learning methods that take robots from understanding 3D scenes to acting on them in real factories: detecting novel and anomalous objects, reasoning about cluttered piles, generating long-horizon motion for surface finishing, and grasping with multiple grippers.

I did my PhD in the VANDAL Lab at Politecnico di Torino and IIT, advised by Prof. Tatiana Tommasi. Today I lead research on Physical AI for industrial surface finishing at mkind in Munich.
